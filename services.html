What We Do

Fitting, service & spares

Everything Sun Auto Gas handles under one roof at Avvai Nagar Main Road, Thiruvanmiyur.

LPG Fitting & Service

Sequential fuel injection LPG kits fitted for petrol cars, with routine servicing to keep the system running clean and efficient. Includes tank, injector rail, wiring harness and ECU setup.

CNG Fitting & Service

CNG conversion for petrol cars, plus after-fitment checks and servicing — cylinder testing, regulator checks and leak testing included as part of routine service visits.

Sequential Fuel Injection Kits

Built for new-generation cars rather than older carburettor-era vehicles — a sequential system meters gas to each cylinder individually for a smoother switch between petrol and gas.

Gas Pipe Line Installation

Gas line and fitting work carried out to standard, as part of a full LPG/CNG fitment or as a standalone job.

All-Brand Kits & Spares

Tanks, injectors, wiring harnesses, regulators and other spares kept in stock across all four tie-up brands, so repairs and servicing don't wait on parts.
