How Fitment Works

From consultation to road-test

  1. Inspection & Consultation

    We check your car's engine and usage to recommend the right kit and tank size.

  2. Tank & Injector Fitting

    Tank, injector rail and wiring are fitted using Lovato, STAG, Zavoli or BRC components.

  3. ECU Mapping & Testing

    The system is mapped and tested for smooth petrol-to-gas switchover.

  4. Road-Test & Handover

    A final road-test before the car goes back to you, ready to run on gas.
