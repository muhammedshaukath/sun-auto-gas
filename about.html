About

We Care Nature

The story behind the signboard on Avvai Nagar Main Road.

Run By P.M. Aziz Ahmed Ansari

A garage, not a chain

Sun Auto Gas is a single garage on Avvai Nagar Main Road (Canal Road), Thiruvanmiyur, run directly by P.M. Ansari. There's no call centre and no franchise layer between you and the person who fits your car — you deal with the same person who does the work.

The shop fits and services sequential LPG and CNG systems for petrol cars, working across all major kit brands rather than pushing one supplier.

Save Money & Save Nature

That line is on the shop's own signboard. LPG and CNG burn cleaner than petrol, so switching isn't only about the running cost — it's a small, direct way to cut what your car puts into the air.
